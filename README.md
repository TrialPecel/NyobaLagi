Rimble is an elegant, minimal, and premium local music player built with Flutter, designed with a focus on modern aesthetics and smooth interactions.

## Features

- **Sleek Dark Mode**: A premium, unified dark aesthetic that is easy on the eyes and makes album art pop.
- **Squircle Album Art**: Modern continuous rectangle "squircle" borders for high-quality album art display.
- **Dynamic Theming**: The app dynamically extracts deep, rich colors from your currently playing song's album art.
- **Unique Radial Dial**: An innovative circular progress dial to track song duration and quickly seek through your music.
- **Gesture Controls**: Swipe left or right on the beautiful carousel to quickly skip tracks.
- **Smart Filtering**: Automatically filters out audio from unwanted folders like WhatsApp, Notifications, and Ringtones.
- **Automated Builds**: Continuous Integration with GitHub Actions automatically builds the latest APK on every commit and publishes it to the Releases section.

## Getting Started

1. Check the **Releases** section on the right side of the GitHub repository to download the latest APK.
2. Install the APK on your Android device.
3. Grant storage permissions when prompted.
4. Enjoy your local music library in style!
